## 9. Scientific Boundaries & Validation Prerequisites

1. Passing sequence off-target screening (`no match detected` or `minimal 19/21 signal`) does **not** prove biological safety.
2. Endogenous hairpin-RNAi knockdown of `CsPKS1` (Leng et al., 2011) does **not** demonstrate exogenous dsRNA uptake or SIGS efficacy.
3. Independent test execution logs (`pytest tests/test_audit_v2.py -v`) are recorded in `BRD_Test_Results_v2.txt`.
4. Priority wet-lab progression: verify exogenous Cy3/FAM-dsRNA uptake in *B. sorokiniana* conidia/hyphae -> qRT-PCR knockdown of `CsSTE11` (`BRD_CsSTE11_CAND012`) & `CsSp1` (`BRD_CsSp1_CAND008`) -> detached leaf infection assays with non-targeting/scrambled dsRNA controls and wheat/barley off-target RT-qPCR verification (checking predicted `20/21` sites for `CsSp1`).
